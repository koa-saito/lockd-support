Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

What we collect

Account: your email address, username, display name and profile color.

Studying: your focus sessions (name or subject, goal, length and time), points, levels, streaks and goals, and the private 1–5 ratings you give sessions.

Social: your friends and friend requests, groups you create or join, likes and comments, and any reports or blocks you make.

Camera: timelapse recordings are made and saved on your device. We don’t upload them.

Who can see what

Friends you accept can see your sessions — name, goal, length, points — plus your streak and totals. Group members can see your username and study totals on group leaderboards. Your ratings, settings and email are only visible to you.

How we use it

Only to run LOCKD: syncing your data, showing your stats, powering friends, groups and leaderboards, and keeping the community safe. We don’t sell your data and we don’t show ads.

Where it’s stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, and protected so each person can only reach what they’re allowed to see.

Deleting your data

Delete your account in Settings → Delete account. Your profile, sessions, points, friendships, comments, likes and the groups you own are removed right away. Reports about content may be kept, without your account attached, so we can keep the community safe.

Children

LOCKD isn’t for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.
