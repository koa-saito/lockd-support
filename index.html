LOCKD Support

LOCKD helps you lock in: time focused study sessions, record timelapses, build streaks and study alongside friends and groups.

Need help or want to report something? Email lockdhelpaddress@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 24 hours, and act on safety reports within 24 hours.

Common questions

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Profile → Settings → Delete account. Your profile, sessions, points, friendships, comments and any groups you own are deleted right away.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap ⋯ on a post in your feed, long-press a comment, or open someone’s profile and choose Report or Block. You can also report a group from its page. Reports are private and reviewed within 24 hours. Manage blocked people in Settings → Blocked people.

Who can see my study sessions?

Only friends you’ve accepted can see your sessions — the name, goal, length and points. Group members see your username and weekly totals. Your ratings, settings and email are private to you.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, type your email and tap “Forgot password?”. We’ll email you a reset link.

Where are my timelapse videos?

Timelapses are recorded and saved on your phone. They aren’t uploaded to LOCKD.
